About

I'm an MSc Cyber Security student at the University of Dubai and an IEEE member. My thesis makes image forensics hold up in the real world, where photos are resized, compressed and cropped before anyone checks them. I combine deep learning, digital watermarking and explainable AI so investigators can see where an image was changed, not just whether it was.

Beyond forensics, I've designed a formally verified key-exchange protocol for 5G-IoT networks and built machine learning tools for phishing detection. I keep my offensive skills sharp on TryHackMe and PortSwigger.

Research

Thesis in progress, expected March 2027

Chain-Aware Tamper Localization under Real-World Image Processing

A transformer-based model (SegFormer MiT-B5 with a U-Net decoder) that pinpoints edited regions even after an image has been resized, JPEG-compressed, cropped and colour-shifted. A processing-chain simulator and a chain-aware FiLM conditioning head give statistically significant gains over single-distortion training.

Pixel AUC
0.96
Twin-F1
0.75
False alarms on authentic images
≈1%

Includes a synthetic forgery dataset (splicing, copy-move, object removal, diffusion inpainting) and a hybrid watermarking extension in progress.

Digital Image Forensics: A Review of Deep Learning and Watermarking Techniques

A review of 26 studies on CNN- and Transformer-based forgery detection and DWT/SVD watermarking. The paper proposes an adaptive forensic framework that pairs watermark authentication with passive AI detection and Grad-CAM explanations.

Gated Spatial-Frequency Fusion for Deepfake Detection

Advanced Digital Forensics, University of Dubai. Paper under review.

A dual-branch PyTorch model that fuses ConvNeXt-Tiny spatial features with Block-DCT frequency artifacts through an adaptive gate. It outperforms six state-of-the-art methods with 98.88% recall, and Grad-CAM shows investigators which facial regions were manipulated.

Privacy-Amplified Security Protocol for 5G-IoT

Network Security, University of Dubai

A lightweight SIGMA-based key exchange using ECDH and AES-GCM for multi-layer 5G-IoT networks. Its authentication and secrecy are formally verified in ProVerif, stateless cookies protect constrained devices from DoS floods, and shuffle-model differential privacy protects aggregated device data.
